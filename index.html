About Virginia Henley

Virginia Henley is a bestselling author of rich, passionate historical romance set in medieval Britain, Renaissance Scotland, and Regency England. Known for meticulous historical detail and memorable characters, her novels transport readers to dramatic eras of love, intrigue, and danger.
